The synth takes over the partial list with harmonics of the pitch. Each amplitude is a glottal source falling at the brightness slope, times four formant resonances F1–F4 (the shape of the throat and mouth). Formants are Peterson & Barney (1952) averages. Type vowels as ee, ih, eh, ae, ah, aw, uu, oo, uh, er, as IPA, or as the key words (heed, hood…).
